## 🕹️ About

My Femboy Roommate: Special Weekend is an adult, choice-driven visual novel and dating sim from Nuteku and Softboi Games. Celebrate a one-year anniversary with Robin at a luxurious resort, explore activities and events, build relationships, and shape the weekend through meaningful decisions. The game is currently in development, and this beta test gives players the opportunity to experience the project early while helping the development team improve it. Beta testers will receive a free game key.

Designed for adults who enjoy LGBTQ+ romance, character-driven visual novels, dating sims, branching choices, and intimate resort stories.

> ⚡ **This is an exclusive beta test opportunity!** Join BETABRIDGE to get early access, help shape the game's development, and earn a free Steam key upon release.

## ✨ Features

- 🏝️ **Luxury Resort Setting** — Explore a high-end resort filled with activities, amenities, and memorable encounters.
- 💞 **Relationship-Focused Story** — Deepen your bond with Robin during an important anniversary weekend.
- 🗣️ **Choice-Driven Narrative** — Make dialogue and activity choices that influence your relationships and story progression.
- 🎮 **Activities and Mini-Games** — Take part in resort events, collectibles, gifts, and interactive mini-games.
- 🎙️ **English Voice Acting** — Experience professionally voiced English dialogue across major story scenes and events.
- 🖼️ **Animated Story Moments** — Unlock animated CGs, additional scenes, multiple endings, and a replay gallery.
